UAE Employment Law

Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 — Leave · Termination · Gratuity · Minors · Equality · Disputes

📅Weekly Rest & Public Holidays

  • At least 1 paid rest day per week
  • Paid leave on all official public holidays (set by Cabinet)
  • Working on a holiday/rest day → substitute day off or basic wage + 50%
  • Overtime: +25%; +50% if between 10 pm – 4 am
  • Max 8 hrs/day, 48 hrs/week; overtime ≤ 2 hrs/day

🌴Annual & Sick Leave

  • Annual: 30 days/year after 1 year
  • 6–12 months service: 2 days per month
  • Paid at basic wage + housing allowance; unused leave paid out on exit
  • Sick (after probation): 90 days/year
    15 full pay · 30 half pay · 45 unpaid
  • Medical report required within 3 days

👨‍👩‍👧Family & Special Leave

  • Maternity: 60 days — 45 full pay + 15 half pay (+45 unpaid if complications)
  • Nursing breaks: 1 hr/day for 6 months
  • Parental: 5 working days within 6 months of birth (mother or father)
  • Bereavement: 5 days spouse · 3 days parent, child, sibling, grandparent
  • Hajj / Umrah: special leave (traditionally up to 30 days once in service, usually unpaid — by agreement)
  • Study: 10 days/year (after 2 years’ service)

📝Termination & Notice

  • Notice period: 30–90 days (written, per contract)
  • Full wage paid during notice, or payment in lieu
  • Employer-issued notice: 1 paid day off/week to job-hunt
  • Instant dismissal only for gross misconduct (Art. 44)
  • Final settlement within 14 days: wages, leave, gratuity, repatriation ticket

💰End-of-Service Gratuity

  • Requires 1+ year continuous service
  • Based on last basic wage only (no allowances)
  • 21 days per year for first 5 years
  • 30 days per year after 5 years
  • Capped at 2 years’ total wage; part-years pro-rata
Daily wage = Basic ÷ 30
Gratuity = Daily × 21 × yrs(≤5)
  + Daily × 30 × yrs(>5)
e.g. AED 9,000 basic, 7 yrs →
300×21×5 + 300×30×2 = AED 49,500

⚖️Protection & Disputes

  • Minors: min. age 15; guardian consent + medical fitness; max 6 hrs/day; no night work (8 pm–6 am), overtime or hazardous jobs
  • Equality: no discrimination by race, colour, sex, religion, origin or disability; equal pay for women
  • Harassment, bullying & forced labour banned
  • MoHRE: file complaint (app / 80084) → amicable settlement; else referred to court
  • MoHRE rules on claims up to AED 50,000; claim within 2 years
