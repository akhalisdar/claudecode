UAE Employment
& Labour Law

Your rights at a glance under Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 — leave, termination, gratuity, minors, equality and disputes.

01

Weekly Rest & Holidays

1daypaid rest per week
+50%for working a holiday
48hmax working week
  • Public holidays are fully paid, as set by the Cabinet
  • Work a holiday or rest day → substitute day off or basic wage + 50%
  • Overtime +25%, or +50% between 10 pm – 4 am
02

Annual & Sick Leave

30daysannual leave after 1 year
90dayssick leave per year
  • 6–12 months: 2 days’ leave per month worked
  • Sick pay: 15 full · 30 half · 45 unpaid (after probation)
  • Unused annual leave is paid out on leaving
03

Family & Special Leave

60daysmaternity
5daysparental
3–5dbereavement
  • Maternity: 45 days full + 15 half pay, plus nursing breaks
  • Bereavement: 5 days spouse, 3 days parent / child / sibling
  • Hajj & Umrah: special leave by agreement (up to 30 days, usually unpaid)
04

Termination & Notice

30–90dwritten notice period
14daysto pay final settlement
  • Full wage during notice, or payment in lieu
  • Settlement covers wages, leave, gratuity & ticket home
  • Dismissal without notice only for gross misconduct (Art. 44)
05

End-of-Service Gratuity

21daysper year, years 1–5
30daysper year after 5
2yrsmaximum cap
  • Needs 1+ year of continuous service
  • Uses last basic wage only, with no allowances
Daily wage = Basic ÷ 30
Example: AED 9,000 basic, 7 yrs
300×21×5 + 300×30×2 = AED 49,500
06

Protection & Disputes

15+minimum working age
6hmax day for minors
2yrsto file a claim
  • Minors: guardian consent, no night, overtime or hazardous work
  • Equality: no discrimination; equal pay for women
  • MoHRE: complain via app / 80084 → settlement or court
